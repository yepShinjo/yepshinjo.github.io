The problem

The part of editing nobody counts

You already watched the ace. You saw the clutch happen. Then you spend the next morning hunting for both — scrubbing, overshooting, scrubbing back, squinting at thumbnails that all show the same corridor. Finding round 14 instead of round 4. Repeat for every highlight in a three-map series.

The watching was the easy part. The finding is what costs you a morning.

The mechanism

Why other timestamp tools break on live streams

Most extensions read the video element's currentTime. On a live stream that number is relative to your buffer — it is not the stream's real position, and it will not match the archived video. Notes taken during the stream are worthless the next day.

Tallylight reads YouTube's player directly for true stream time: the same offsets the published video will have. Mark it live, and it still lines up when you sit down to edit.

How it works

Four steps, then you are marking

01
Open a live streamAny YouTube live stream. Recorded videos work too.
02
Tap Ctrl at each momentA toast confirms every mark. Combos like Ctrl+C are ignored, and so is typing in live chat.
03
Label during quiet stretches"round 4 start", "ace — Jett", "clutch 1v3". Far easier now than from memory tomorrow.
04
Export when the stream endsCopy the log as text, or export CSV with exact seconds and a link per mark.

Details

▸

Policy

Privacy

Last updated 24 August 2026

Tallylight stores your timestamps in your own browser. It does not send them anywhere. There is no analytics, no tracking, and no account.

What is stored

  • Your marks — the timestamp, any label you type, the video ID and title, and the clock time of each mark.
  • Your settings — chosen hotkey and back-date value.

All of it is written to your browser's local extension storage, on your own device. None of it is transmitted to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it.

What is read

On YouTube pages only, Tallylight reads the current playback position and the video's ID and title from YouTube's player. It reads nothing else on the page, and it does not run on any other website.

What we do not do

  • No analytics or telemetry
  • No advertising or advertising identifiers
  • No selling or sharing of data
  • No accounts, profiles, or browsing-history collection
  • No transmission of your marks off your device

Payments

Pro is sold through Dodo Payments, our merchant of record, who processes the payment and issues your licence key. They receive your email address and payment details in order to do that. We never see or store your payment details.

When you activate Pro, the extension sends only your licence key, an activation ID and a generic browser label to Dodo Payments' licensing service to confirm it is valid, and re-checks it every few days. No timestamps, labels, or video information are ever sent with it.

Permissions

  • Storage — to save your marks and settings on your device.
  • Access to youtube.com — to read playback position on the page you are watching, and to show the confirmation when you mark a moment.
  • Access to the licensing API — contacted only when you activate a licence key, to confirm it is valid.

Deleting your data

Open Tallylight's Settings page and use Delete all marks. Removing the extension deletes its stored data as well.

Children

Tallylight is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information.
